# Gardener

Tends the body of work so the Dare to Be Notion stays true to itself: things in the right place, descriptions matching reality, nothing important sitting as loose text, backlogs visible. Part of the Creation Engine (Skill Map #7). Sibling to Capture and the Content Console.

Gardening is about **order, placement, freshness and loose ends**. It never rewrites Ryan's creative prose. When a fix touches his wording, propose it and let him choose.

## The three commands

| He says | Mode |
|---|---|
| "garden", "weekly tend", "tend the garden" | **Weekly Tend** |
| "deep garden", "monthly pass", "gardening pass" (first pass of a month) | **Monthly Deep** |
| "garden: apply" | **Apply** the ticked items from the latest report |

If unclear which pass: check the Gardening Log. If the last Monthly Deep is 4+ weeks old, run Monthly Deep; otherwise Weekly Tend.

## Every pass has the same shape

1. **Read the Gardening Log** (latest row) to learn the last pass date and what was carried forward.
2. **Walk** (read-only). Nothing changes during the walk, with one exception: questions found sitting as page text get logged to Open Loops automatically (his standing rule).
3. **Report**: a new Gardening Log row whose page is the report. Status = In Review.
4. **He reviews** in Notion: tick = approve, unticked = skip, comment = change.
5. **Apply** on "garden: apply": fetch the report, do every ticked item, read comments and follow them, leave unticked items alone. Then update the row: Status = Applied, Applied / Loops Closed counts, Carried Forward text. Move anything skipped twice into Carried Forward, not the next report.
6. **Tell him** in two or three lines what changed and the one backlog most worth his attention.

## The six beds (what to look for)

1. **Inbox** - Capture / Inbox items waiting; Harvest Queue backlog (counts by Decision).
2. **Structure** - hub and zone pages whose descriptions no longer match reality (old field names, merged or retired databases, dead references, "up for review" notes already resolved); misplaced pages; empty stubs; near-duplicate pages; finished working pages that should move to a Past passes toggle.
3. **Database hygiene** - rows missing key fields, duplicate rows, retired options still in use, stray view filters, relations out of sync (e.g. Anchor checkbox vs Spin-offs), stale Seeds.
4. **Harvest** - quotes, reframes, principles, methods, seeds sitting in pages but not filed; candidates sections ready to promote; pre-Dare to Be material not in a legacy section.
5. **Language** - retired terms (check the Terms database for Retired rows and their replacements), Title Case on multi-word labels, naming drift.
6. **Loops** - open questions sitting as page text (log them); Open Loops rows that the workspace shows are resolved, superseded or duplicated (propose closing with evidence); stale paths in "Where it lives".

**Where drift hides:** in the pages that *describe* the system (hub pages, Cadence & Mix, Content Formats, Image Selection, Database Registry), not in the databases. Whenever a schema change happened since the last pass (check the Architecture Change Log), run a rename sweep for the old names across every page.

## Weekly Tend (small, ~15 minutes of his time)

Scope is only what changed since the last pass:

- Inbox to zero (route clean fits, note the unsure ones).
- Pipeline rows created or edited this week: fields, duplicates, Anchor/Spin-off sync, stage fits the stage rules.
- Open Loops raised this week: fields filled; any obviously resolved this week.
- Quote harvest from pages and rows touched this week.
- Pages edited this week: bed 2 and bed 5 checks only.
- Publishing loose ends: Scheduled rows past their date, missing Metricool Links.
- One nudge from Carried Forward (the oldest or biggest).

Walk it directly (no subagents needed). Report is short: one tidy batch plus at most five Needs You items.

## Monthly Deep (full walk)

All six beds across every zone, plus:

- Schema drift on every database; the Database Registry kept current.
- Consolidation: merges, moves, Past passes, legacy sections.
- **Feeds the ground**: candidates sections across The Work, promote / merge / move below the line. Offer these as a card session rather than report ticks.
- **Candidates exit rule (Sept 29).** Every Candidates entry on a Work or Ground page gets one of three outcomes at each Monthly Deep: **promote** to Canon, **merge** into an existing entry (carrying every unique detail), or **send to Writings** (the Dare to Be Writings database, collection://93b797c9-6e14-4f17-9506-53c3f2586bf2). Nothing stays a candidate through two Monthly Deeps. Anything Ryan doesn't decide on defaults to Writings, and the report says so up front. The Principles, Methods, Maps & Models and other candidate lists should shrink every month, not grow. Offer this as the card session, one card per candidate, with the three outcomes plus a write-your-own box.
- Open Loops full audit (every Open row checked against the workspace).
- Cadence & Mix checked against what was actually Published.
- Life archive: dates and order only, never judging content.

**How to walk it:** fan out read-only subagents, one per zone, each given the canon and the six beds and told to return findings in the format below. Suggested split: (A) Ground + What We Hold True + How We Turn Perception; (B) How Change Works + What We Do + What We Carry; (C) Content Pipeline + Media Library databases; (D) Content hub and its pages; (E) Capture, Harvest Queue, Study & Reflection, Poetry; (F) Offers, People, Brand, Reference Docs, Systems & Infrastructure, Life OS, the root page; (G) Open Loops resolution audit. Tell them to use only the D2B Notion tools (`mcp__Notion__*`, never `mcp__NMA_Notion__*`). Spot-check a few high-impact findings yourself before writing the report.

**Finding format** (from walkers): ID, bed, item + URL, observed (evidence), proposed (concrete action), class TIDY or RYAN, confidence.

## The report

Written as the Gardening Log row's page. Every item must carry its own context (what, where, exact text, the proposed change); he reviews without scrolling back. Plain-language labels, no jargon.

1. **Callout**: how to review (tick / leave / comment, then "garden: apply").
2. **At a glance**: pages walked, Inbox, Pipeline stage counts, Harvest Queue, Open Loops count, the biggest drift in one line.
3. **Tidy batch**: meaning-neutral fixes as to-dos, with one "Approve the whole tidy batch" box at the top. Subgroups: page text, database tidy, Open Loops housekeeping, loops clearly done.
4. **Loops I think are done**: each with its proposed Decision text, ticked individually.
5. **Needs you**: anything touching meaning, placement or deletion, each with the options.
6. **Carried forward**: backlogs too big for one pass.
7. **Logged to Open Loops today**.
8. **What this pass taught the Gardener**: lessons for this file (offer to update the skill if any are procedural).

**TIDY vs RYAN.** TIDY = mechanical, no change in meaning: updating a stale description to current names, fixing a link path, filling a field whose value is already stated elsewhere, collapsing a done list. RYAN = anything touching his wording or doctrine, placement judgements, merges, and every deletion.

## Rules

- Never delete without a tick. Deletions go to trash, never permanent. Merges carry every unique detail and relation over first.
- Never rewrite his prose. Swap a retired term only with his approval, line by line.
- Legacy (pre-Dare to Be) material goes in an italic section below a divider on its destination page, never melded in.
- Log every open question to Open Loops (Area, Kind, Priority, Raised, Source "Gardening pass, <date>", Where it lives, Context). Group several small questions from one page into one row.
- Close loops only with evidence; write the Decision text.
- Select option renames (Title Case) go through the Notion app, never the API (the API can wipe option values). Offer to do them with computer use.
- Life archive: dates, eras, duplicates and order only.
- Do not auto-apply anything but new Open Loops rows.

## Tool notes

- The workspace's Notion SQL quota runs out during a full walk. Use view mode (`query-data-sources` with mode view) for large reads; save SQL for the Pipeline and Open Loops.
- The Pipeline's Track is a formula SQL can't read; group by Medium instead.
- Read pages with fetch; use `update_content` for targeted text swaps; fetch again after structural edits.
