# Content pillars

**Status:** Open. Not urgent.

Something is wanted for cadence beyond Form and Medium, describing what a creator offers rather than what they conclude. A set of themes named after the claims pieces make was proposed and rejected as pigeonholing.

Direction so far: pillars exist as a field in the shared taxonomy, but each creator's values live in their profile.

Do not resolve unilaterally. Raise it when it becomes load-bearing.
