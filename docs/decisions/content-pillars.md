# Content pillars

**Status:** Partly settled, Sept 28. A **Pillars** field now exists on the Pipeline as the start of content categories. Dare to Be's values (Somatics, Soul, Story, Strategy, Systems) live in its profile. Still open: how Pillars feed cadence.

Something is wanted for cadence beyond Shape and Medium, describing what a creator offers rather than what they conclude. A set of themes named after the claims pieces make was proposed and rejected as pigeonholing.

Direction so far: pillars exist as a field in the shared taxonomy, but each creator's values live in their profile.

Do not resolve unilaterally. Raise it when it becomes load-bearing.
