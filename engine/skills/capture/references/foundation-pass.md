# The foundation pass

Ryan asked for this in his own words: when he's sharing something, there's often a philosophy, a creed line or a quote deeper inside it, connected to his core values. Those things are foundational and don't need updating all the time, so the pass should recognise where they might go.

Run it on every unit, alongside the content pass. Ask: what does this point at underneath?

## What to look for

Use the structure of The Architecture of the Teaching, which the Dare to Be Notion follows (The Work holds five groups):

- **What We Hold True**: premise, creed, principles, orientations. A premise says why the work exists ("I'm here to help people change how they behave, not just how they think"). A principle is a truth that guides action ("joy is a prerequisite, not a reward"). An orientation is a stance you take toward life ("individuated unity").
- **How We Turn Perception**: distinctions (two things people confuse, named apart: maintenance vs. creation; egoic vs. surrendered sovereignty) and reframes.
- **How Change Works**: maps and models, mechanisms (why a breakthrough fades: the predictive pattern isn't updated by the lived experience).
- **What We Do**: tools, practices, methods (the flinch diagnostic; catharsis's three steps).
- **What We Carry**: lexicon (unguarded, the old gravity, the bodyguard), images and metaphors (two suns).

Also watch for **hungers** (for The Potent Person) and **brand premises** (why a soul mentor talks about AI).

## Where candidates go

Not straight into The Work. The ground is kept distilled on purpose: a 30-line creed means nothing (his metal-element principle: refine, distil, let go). So:

- New candidates go into a candidates section at the bottom of their home page, marked for the monthly deep sweep.
- Additions that merely deepen an existing line (same idea, new wording) are merge notes in that section.
- If he approves a refinement as stated ("true, effortless joy is a state, not an emotion"), file it and mark it "filed with your yes".
- The monthly deep sweep (Content Console Garden mode) is where he decides what earns a permanent place and what to let go.

## Useful habits

- One image per idea. When two metaphors describe the same thing (two suns; a pendulum no longer swinging), file both and note the choice.
- Name what's new. If a candidate is 90% already on file, the card says so and it becomes a merge.
- Answer his placement questions directly on the card. On the first run he asked where the sovereignty idea sits. The answer: the stance (individuated unity) is an orientation, egoic vs. surrendered sovereignty is a distinction, and the creed-level truth underneath both is closer to "we are completed by what we belong to."
- Watch borrowed phrases. "Power vs. force" is also a book title; say so.
