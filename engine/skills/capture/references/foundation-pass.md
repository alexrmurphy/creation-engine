# The foundation pass

When a creator shares something, there's often a philosophy, a creed line or a quote deeper inside it, connected to their core values. Those things are foundational and don't need updating all the time, so the pass recognises where they might go.

Run it on every unit, alongside the content pass. Ask: what does this point at underneath?

## What to look for

The kinds below are the same for every creator. The profile's `capture.md` says how the creator's foundation pages are grouped and which page each kind lives on.

- **Premise**: why the work exists. "I'm here to help people change how they act, not just how they think."
- **Creed and principles**: truths that guide action. "Rest is part of the work, not a reward for it."
- **Orientations**: stances taken toward life.
- **Distinctions**: two things people confuse, named apart. "Discipline vs. rigidity."
- **Reframes**: a move from one way of seeing to another.
- **Maps, models and mechanisms**: how change works, and why something happens. "Why a breakthrough fades: the old habit was never updated by the new experience."
- **Tools, practices and methods**: what the creator has people do.
- **Lexicon, images and metaphors**: the creator's own terms and the pictures they think in.

Also watch for **audience hungers** (pains or longings in the people they serve) and **brand premises** (why this creator talks about this subject at all).

## Where candidates go

Not straight into the creator's doctrine pages. The ground is kept distilled on purpose: a 30-line creed means nothing. So:

- New candidates go into a candidates section at the bottom of their home page, under the candidates heading from the dials, marked for the monthly deep sweep.
- Additions that merely deepen an existing line (same idea, new wording) are merge notes in that section.
- If the creator approves a refinement as stated, file it and mark it "filed with your yes".
- The monthly deep sweep is where they decide what earns a permanent place and what to let go.

Each candidate: bold name, their exact words in quotes, one line on what it is, links to the Pipeline pieces that carry it.

## Useful habits

- One image per idea. When two metaphors describe the same thing, file both and note the choice.
- Name what's new. If a candidate is 90% already on file, the card says so and it becomes a merge.
- Answer placement questions directly on the card. When the creator asks where an idea sits, the answer often splits it: the stance is an orientation, the contrast inside it is a distinction, and the truth underneath both is a creed-level line. Say so.
- Watch borrowed phrases. If a phrase is also a book title or someone else's signature line, say so.
