# Stages for captured pieces

The same for every creator, and the same definitions the Content Console uses. The profile's `capture.md` may point to examples from the creator's own runs.

**Seed**: a concept without a forming shape. More of an idea, or a thing the creator is doing, than a piece.

**Sprout**: raw material. It can be long, loose and channelled, a lot of words, and still be a Sprout. A piece with enough for a draft stays a Sprout until the shape has been reviewed and confirmed.

**Draft**: actually drafted. Dictated close to postable, needing only the Console's refinement pass.

**Merge**: an existing Pipeline row receives the new material. If the addition gives it a visible shape, move it up (Seed to Sprout, for example).

## What a Sprout carries

Everything the creator said that connects to it, from anywhere in the capture, in their words. If they returned to a topic later in the capture, both passages go on the row, labelled as passes ("First pass", "Second pass"). Plus: links to neighbours, the foundation candidates it carries, the open questions before drafting.

## When they ask for a Draft

If the shape isn't confirmed yet, file at Sprout with everything gathered, note "draft-ready once X is settled", and say so in one line. They'll draft it through the Console.

## Anchors

Mark Anchor when the creator calls something an anchor post or it could run long (thousands of words) with other pieces spinning off it. Spin-offs are separate rows with their own titles, linked to the anchor.

## Shapes

Name a Shape only when it's visible in the material; leave it empty on a Seed otherwise. Use the Pattern Book's forms, the same names as the Pipeline's Shape options. Mention the Shape in plain words on the card ("Shape: Object").
