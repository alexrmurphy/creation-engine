# Stages for captured pieces

Ryan's rule, set during the first capture run (27 Sept 2026):

**Seed**: a concept without a forming shape. "More of just a concept or a thing I'm doing." Example: The knowledge base.

**Sprout**: raw material. It can be long, loose and channelled, a lot of words, and still be a Sprout. A piece with enough for a draft stays a Sprout until the shape has been reviewed and confirmed. Examples: I've officially left the cult of sovereignty (two full passes of channelled material); AI: more yourself or less (loose and channelling); The drum and The urn (the Object shape is visible).

**Draft**: actually drafted. Dictated close to postable, needing only the Console's refinement pass. Example: Confession: almost everything I share is touched by AI.

**Merge**: an existing Pipeline row receives the new material. If the addition gives it a visible shape, move it up (The old gravity went from Seed to Sprout).

## What a Sprout carries

Everything he channelled that connects to it, from anywhere in the capture, in his words. If he returned to a topic later ("a little bit more about the cult of sovereignty peace"), both passages go on the row, labelled as passes. Plus: links to neighbours, the foundation candidates it carries, the open questions before drafting.

## When he asks for a Draft

If the shape isn't confirmed yet, file at Sprout with everything gathered, note "draft-ready once X is settled", and say so in one line. He'll draft it through the Console.

## Anchors

Mark `Anchor` when he calls something an anchor post or it could run long (thousands of words) with other pieces spinning off it. Spin-offs are separate rows with their own titles, linked to the anchor.

## Shapes

Name a Shape only when it's visible in the material; leave it empty on a Seed otherwise. Use The Pattern Book's forms, the same names as the Pipeline's Shape options. Mention the Shape in plain words on the card ("Shape: Object").
