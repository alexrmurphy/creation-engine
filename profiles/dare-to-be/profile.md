## Voice rules

1. **No em dashes.** His dash is a spaced hyphen, his pause is an ellipsis.
2. **Draft rhythm.** Mostly short paragraphs with breaks, occasional single lines, short runs of three or four stacked lines. No big blocks. Not everything as single sentences. No clipped-fragment AI rhythm.
3. **One reframe per piece, in a full sentence.** Stacked rhetorical antithesis is not his voice.
4. **Retired terms** never appear in new work. See Foundations Part X.

## Conventions

- **Feedback marker:** `note -` in his feedback means a question or something needing a bigger rewrite, not an answer.
- **Raw original section:** called **As channelled**. A collapsed section on the row holding his original unedited dictation.
- **Quote harvest destination:** Content / Quotes / My Quotes, filed by theme.

## Creator traits

- **He channels by voice, which keeps him in philosophy and generalities.** Scene detail is what gets lost. This is structural, not occasional, and it is why the specificity rule matters so much for him.
- **Specificity sources:** Life / Ryan's Life, Life / Currents, the origin scenes in Foundations Part I, and the Pipeline archive.
- **Composite model:** the "it's the kid in school who learned to be the class clown" move in Foundations Part 7 is the model for second-person and composite scenes.
- **Levity:** he usually has some. His own material is the first place to look for it.

## Doctrine

- **What the piece is doing: the fish ladder.** Awakening the desire, giving a fish, inspiring the fisherman, teaching to fish, or off-spine. Descriptive, never a gate. Off-spine pieces are legitimate. See Cadence & Mix, axis 5. Not to be confused with the specificity ladder, where "rung" means altitude.
- **Audience hungers:** The Work / The Potent Person. When a piece is awakening the desire, name which hunger it points at.
- **Cadence & Mix:** five axes, read live in Notion.
