## Ryan's corrections, now rules

- **Sprout vs. Draft.** He asked for the sovereignty piece as a Draft, then on the next card said a piece with enough for a draft should wait at Sprout until the shape is reviewed. Applied to both. Now the stage rule.
- **Bring everything in.** For loose channelled pieces he's fine with Sprout "as long as we're bringing in absolutely everything that's connected".
- **Seed is for concepts.** The knowledge base had no forming shape, so Seed.
- **Precision on foundations.** "Joy is a state" became "true, effortless joy is a state, not an emotion; there is also the emotion of joy."
- **Cut open loops he's handling elsewhere** (text-on-screen videos, feedback hub, manager agents, Qigong naming, architecture restructure, engine naming, maps vs. models). Don't re-propose them from later captures unless he raises them again.
